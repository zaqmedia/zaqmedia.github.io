FAQ

Before you ask.

Anything else, send us a message. We reply within a day.

How much does a video cost in Ethiopia with ZAQ Media?

Brand films start at ETB 80,000, social media content at ETB 55,000 a month and TV commercials at ETB 195,000. After a short discovery call we send a fixed quote, so you know the full cost before anything is shot.

How long does a video project take?

A starter brand film takes 7–14 business days. Real estate films take 5–10 business days. Campaigns take 2–4 weeks and documentaries 4–8 weeks, depending on shoot days.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every project. Extra rounds beyond the agreed scope are priced together with you before we start them.

Do you film outside Addis Ababa?

Yes. We film across Ethiopia and travel further for the right project. Any travel and logistics costs are agreed upfront.

Can you produce for international brands shooting in Ethiopia?

Yes. We handle location scouting, permits, local cast and crew, the shoot itself and post-production, plus the cultural and logistical advice that makes a shoot run smoothly.

Can you invoice in ETB?

Yes. Local clients are invoiced in ETB. International clients can be invoiced in USD.
